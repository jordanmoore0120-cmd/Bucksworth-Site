### Google Indexing + AI Mode Insights (Jul 16, 2026)

**Source:** [How to Overcome Google Indexing Problems With Social Media + AI](https://whitespark.ca/blog/how-to-overcome-google-indexing-problems-with-social-media-ai/)

**#24: Social media as AI indexing bypass** — When Google refuses to index web pages, Google AI Mode and ChatGPT still surface social media content for the same queries, often within 1-2 days of posting. AI Mode doesn't require a page to be indexed — it scrapes social. *Action: Post about every Bucksworth service on social media (FB, IG, Google Posts) so AI Mode surfaces us for service queries even if a new page isn't indexed yet.*

**#25: 7-step workflow for indexing problems** — If Google won't index a page: (1) Define priority topics, (2) Research those topics in AI Mode, (3) Document which social platforms are cited, (4) Check those platforms' community policies, (5) Publish genuinely helpful content there with links where allowed, (6) Cover every topic on BOTH website AND social, (7) Run same queries on ChatGPT — it often links directly to the site when AI Mode won't. *Action: For any new Bucksworth service page that takes >2 weeks to index, create matching social posts immediately.*

**#26: ChatGPT vs AI Mode** — ChatGPT links directly to websites more often than AI Mode. AI Mode relies heavily on social. For local businesses: optimize for both. Social feeds AI Mode; strong pages feed ChatGPT. *Action: Track Bucksworth mentions in both AI Mode and ChatGPT periodically.*
