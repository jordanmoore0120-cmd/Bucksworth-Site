11. **Keywords in title tag** of landing page linked from GBP

20. **Service area pages on website**

## 4. CONTENT STRATEGY RULES (from Darren Shaw 2025-2026)

### Service Area Pages
- Duplicate your core service page for each neighborhood/city you serve
- NOT copy-paste — rework each paragraph, ideally in business owner's voice
- Include: local references, neighborhood names, zip codes, landmarks
- Each page should stand on its own as genuinely useful content
- Internal link all service area pages to the hub/pillar page
- "Pillar page → spoke pages" architecture

### Blog Content
- **June 2026 shift:** Darren Shaw says "Ditch 'how-to' blogs for high-impact portfolio content"
  - Show PROOF of work done (before/after photos, case studies)
  - Portfolio content > generic advice content
  - BUT: localized how-to content still valuable for relevance signals and AI extraction
- Content should be "excellent and readable and people are consuming it"
- Behavioral signals (time on page, engagement) matter — content MUST be genuinely useful
- Word count: "look at what pages are ranked — if they have 1,000 words, match that ballpark"
- Service pages: "less about tons of content, more about good, clear content — get right to the point"
- Blog/location pages: can be longer, more comprehensive

### Localized Content Requirements
- Match website pages to GBP services (every GBP service should have a corresponding page)
- Service pages should answer every question someone could ask
- Include: pricing, specific brands/products, service details, FAQs
- "Tell people everything you can about your business"
- Entity optimization: consistently describe WHAT you are, WHAT you do, WHERE you serve

### Content for Multi-City Ranking
- Build city hub pages: `/{city}/` as pillar
- Build service pages per city: `/{city}/{service}/`
- Build sub-service pages per city: `/{city}/{service}/{sub-service}/`
- Internal link everything: hub → service → sub-service (and back up)
- Get localized links TO your city hub pages (chambers, sponsorships, local orgs)
- Get reviews mentioning those cities
- Blog content per city reinforces relevance

## 5. REVIEW STRATEGY (from Darren Shaw 2025-2026)

### Core Rules
- **Recency > Volume** — consistent stream of reviews beats old bulk
- Ask EVERY customer for a review (must be systematic, not sporadic)
- Encourage customers to **mention the service** in their review text ("keywords in reviews")
- Mix review platforms: Google (primary), Yelp, Facebook, BBB, industry-specific
- **Local Guides** reviews may carry more weight — give extra attention to Local Guide customers
- Review velocity matters: Google looks for consistent cadence, not bursts
- Photos in reviews add extra value
- Respond to ALL reviews, including anonymous ones

### Darren Shaw's Review Diversification Strategy
- Primary: ~60% to Google
- Secondary: ~20% to Yelp (because Yelp buys Google ad space for home services)
- Tertiary: ~10% to Facebook
- Remaining: ~10% to industry-specific (HomeAdvisor, Angi, BBB)
- DON'T send all reviews to Google — diversification is a ranking factor

### Review Solicitation Best Practices
- Use a handout/email with step-by-step instructions
- Make it as easy as possible (direct link to review form)
- Time the ask: right after service completion when satisfaction is highest
- For multi-location: route reviews to the correct GBP location

## 6. CITATION STRATEGY (from Darren Shaw 2025-2026)

### 2026 Citation Renaissance (driven by AI)
AI search is bringing citations back into importance. Key citation factors:
1. **Presence on expert-curated "best of" lists** — highest impact
2. **Prominence on key industry-relevant domains** (FindLaw, ZocDoc equivalents)
3. **Quality/authority of unstructured citations** (news, blogs, associations)
4. **Review authority of third-party sites** (reviews on high-authority platforms)
5. **Quantity of unstructured citations** (PR reach)
6. **Diversity of review platforms**
7. **Quantity of unlinked brand mentions**
8. **Industry-specific directory citations**

### Citation Description Optimization (Darren Shaw's "Slept-On Tactic")
- Most businesses set up directory listings once and never touch descriptions
- Go through ALL directory listings and UPDATE the descriptions
- Make descriptions entity-optimized: what you are, what you do, WHERE you serve
- Mention all service areas in directory descriptions
- This is a high-ROI activity — low effort, meaningful ranking impact
- "Taking a sweep through your citations and updating the description is one way you can improve relevancy signals for other areas you want to rank in"

### Key Directories (Priority Order for Home Services)
1. Google Business Profile ✅
2. Yelp — CRITICAL (buys Google ad space)
3. Facebook Business Page
4. BBB
5. Apple Maps
6. Bing Maps
7. Yellow Pages / Superpages
8. Foursquare / Swarm
9. Data-Axle / Neustar
10. Industry-specific: Angi, HomeAdvisor, Thumbtack
11. Local: Chamber of Commerce, city directories

## 7. LINK BUILDING STRATEGY (from Darren Shaw 2025-2026)

### Key Insight: Behavioral > Links (but links still matter)
- "You can have great content with no links, but it's being shared in Slack groups, on emails, it's getting all the engagement behavioral signals and that's enough"
- BUT: for local, links from LOCAL sources > links from national sources
- Citation-level links (directories) are baseline — competitors have them too
- To rank BETTER, you need better links above the baseline

### Link Types (Priority Order)
1. **Sponsorship links** — sponsor local teams, events, charities
   - Search: `{city name} intitle:sponsors` or `{city name} inurl:sponsors`
   - Get a blurb about your business + link
   - Ideal: anchor text = `{city} {service}` (but usually can't control)
2. **Chamber of Commerce** memberships
3. **Local organization** memberships (Rotary, BNI, etc.)
4. **Guest posts** on local blogs/news
5. **PR / news mentions** — local media coverage
6. **Industry directory** listings
7. **Meetup/event** hosting or sponsorship

### Multi-City Link Strategy
- Get localized links TO your city hub pages (not just homepage)
- Example: Denver Chamber link → your Denver hub page
- Internal linking then distributes authority to service pages beneath

## 8. GBP OPTIMIZATION (from Darren Shaw 2025-2026)

### Priority Order
1. **Primary category** — most closely aligned with primary ranking goal
2. **Business name** — must match real-world name (don't spam keywords)
3. **Additional categories** — add all relevant ones
4. **Services** — fill out with descriptions, match to website pages
5. **Products** — fill out even for service businesses (high visibility)
6. **Attributes** — all applicable
7. **Website URL** → homepage (for primary keywords)
8. **Photos + Videos** — upload continuously (not a one-time thing)
9. **Google Posts** — 1/week minimum, conversion-focused copy
10. **Description** — write as conversion copy, not keyword stuffing
11. **Hours** — accurate, expanded when possible
12. **Q&A** — deprecated on desktop, seed questions as Posts instead

### GBP Description Strategy
- NOT for ranking (description doesn't directly impact ranking)
- Write for CONVERSION — "What would convince someone to pick up the phone?"
- Include: service overview, competitive differentiators, trust signals

### GBP Posts Strategy
- Minimum 1/week
- Use best reviews as post content
- Repurpose testimonials as posts
- Make each post "compelling and explain why you're awesome"
- Include strong CTA

### Services Section (Hot in 2026)
- Services jumped significantly in ranking importance
- Add descriptions (most businesses don't — competitive advantage)
- Match every GBP service to a website page
- Consider emojis in service titles (get pulled into local pack "Provides:" feature)
- Fill out ALL services across ALL categories

## 9. SURROUNDING CITIES / SERVICE AREA EXPANSION (from Whitespark Guide + Darren Shaw)

### The Reality
- **< 20 miles from GBP**: possible to rank with strong signals
- **> 20 miles from GBP**: practically need a new physical location
- Service area settings in GBP DO NOT impact rankings (confirmed by Darren Shaw)
- To rank in surrounding cities, you need to overcome the proximity disadvantage

### How to Rank in Surrounding Cities (Without New Location)
1. **Service area pages** on website for each target city
2. **Localized content** (blog posts) mentioning target city + neighborhoods
3. **Localized links** from target city sources (chambers, sponsorships, organizations)
4. **Reviews from customers in those cities** — "the dream"
5. **Unstructured citations** mentioning you + target city
6. **Citation description optimization** — mention all service areas in directory descriptions
7. **Internal linking** — city hub pages linked to from all related content

### AI Overviews Opportunity
- AI Overviews are DILUTING proximity bias
- Content quality and comprehensiveness matters more for AI results
- Businesses with excellent content can appear in AI results for cities further away
- This is the window of opportunity — while AI search is young

### What Won't Work
- Just setting service area in GBP
- Generic service area pages with only city name changed
- Thin content that's obviously templated

## 11. WHAT NOT TO WASTE TIME ON (from Darren Shaw 2026)

- ❌ Minor NAP inconsistencies (suite number formatting, etc.) — "little details are super not important"
- ❌ Keyword stuffing in GBP description (doesn't impact ranking)
- ❌ Setting service area in GBP (doesn't impact ranking)
- ❌ Obsessing over social media for ranking purposes (< 1% signal weight)
- ❌ Generic how-to blogs with no local angle (replaced by AI answers)
- ❌ One-time citation buildout without description optimization
- ❌ Review gating (filtering who you ask) — ask EVERYONE
- ❌ Buying links from non-local sources
