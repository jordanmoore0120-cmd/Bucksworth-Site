# E-E-A-T Checklist — Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness

## Experience Signals
- [ ] First-person voice ("we"/"our") on all service and blog pages
- [ ] Reference "our technicians" or "our team" naturally
- [ ] Include specific experience: "Serving Arizona since 2013"
- [ ] Real project descriptions (not stock/generic)
- [ ] City-specific knowledge (neighborhoods, landmarks, local conditions)
- [ ] Seasonal expertise (monsoon pest behavior, AC demand patterns)

## Expertise Signals
- [ ] Author attribution: "Jordan Moore, Founder & CEO" on blog posts
- [ ] AZ ROC license (#343924) and AZ Dept of Agriculture license (#9613) on pest/weed pages
- [ ] EPA-approved treatments mention for pest content
- [ ] Technical accuracy (correct product names, proper terminology)
- [ ] Explain cost FACTORS only; never quote prices, ranges, deals or contract terms (Jordan rule)

## Authoritativeness Signals
- [ ] Google Guaranteed badge
- [ ] BBB accreditation
- [ ] Review count and rating on Google (currently building)
- [ ] Backlinks from authoritative sources (PR engine goal: DR 30+ sites)
- [ ] Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across all citations
- [ ] Industry association memberships (NPMA, local chamber)

## Trustworthiness Signals
- [ ] Real phone numbers on every page (PHX: 480-422-8388, TUC: 520-284-9930)
- [ ] Physical address on homepage and contact page
- [ ] Privacy policy and terms of service
- [ ] Secure site (HTTPS — already on Vercel)
- [ ] No misleading claims or fake urgency
- [ ] Clear service area boundaries (we say where we DON'T serve too)
- [ ] Customer reviews integrated into content (not just a reviews page)
- [ ] Satisfaction guarantee stated clearly

## Per-Page Checklist (automated by content scorer)
Every city/service page MUST have:
1. City name in title, H1, and first paragraph
2. At least one zip code reference
3. At least one neighborhood mention
4. Phone number for the correct branch
5. Link to relevant service page
6. Link to city landing page
7. At least one trust signal (license, guarantee, reviews)
8. Specific pricing or "free estimate" CTA
9. Author or company attribution
10. Last-modified date in structured data
