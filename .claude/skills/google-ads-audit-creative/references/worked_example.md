# Worked Example: GlowLab Creative Audit

Fictional DTC skincare brand "GlowLab" selling through their Shopify store. Account runs Search (3 ad groups), PMax (2 asset groups), and YouTube (1 awareness campaign). Account maturity: Developing.

---

## Account Overview

- **Business:** DTC skincare, Shopify store
- **Monthly spend:** $15,000 (Search $6K, PMax $7K, YouTube $2K)
- **Campaign types:** Search (3 ad groups), PMax (2 asset groups), YouTube (1 campaign)
- **Maturity:** Developing (50-100 conversions/month, basic tracking in place)

---

## Step 2: RSA Evaluation

### Ad Group: "Moisturizers"

**Asset counts:**
- Headlines: 5 of 15 recommended (Critical: below 8 minimum)
- Descriptions: 2 of 4 recommended (OK: meets minimum)

**Ad Strength:** Poor

**Asset ratings:** 2 Learning, 2 Good, 1 Low

**Pin usage:** All 3 headline positions pinned to single assets. This RSA is effectively a static ad with no optimization flexibility.

**Headline diversity:** Weak (1 category)
- All 5 headlines are variations of "Shop Moisturizers" with slight wording changes
- No keyword-specific headlines, no CTAs, no benefit language, no social proof
- Example headlines: "Shop Our Moisturizers," "Buy Moisturizers Online," "Moisturizer Sale," "Best Moisturizers," "Shop Moisturizers Now"

**Finding:** This ad group needs a complete RSA overhaul. Add 10 new headlines across multiple diversity categories. Remove all pins. Replace the Low-rated asset.

### Ad Group: "Anti-Aging Serums"

**Asset counts:**
- Headlines: 12 of 15 recommended (OK)
- Descriptions: 4 of 4 recommended (OK)

**Ad Strength:** Good

**Asset ratings:** 3 Best, 4 Good, 3 Learning, 2 Low

**Pin usage:** Brand name pinned to H1, everything else unpinned (appropriate pattern)

**Headline diversity:** Strong (6 categories covered)
- Keywords: "Anti-Aging Serum," "Retinol Serum"
- Benefits: "Reduce Fine Lines in 2 Weeks"
- Social proof: "Rated 4.8/5 by 3,000 Customers"
- CTA: "Try Risk-Free for 30 Days"
- Brand: "GlowLab Anti-Aging"
- Offer: "Free Shipping Over $50"

**Finding:** Strong RSA. Replace the 2 Low-rated assets. Add 3 more headlines to reach 15. No structural changes needed.

### Ad Group: "Sunscreen SPF"

**Asset counts:**
- Headlines: 10 of 15 recommended (OK)
- Descriptions: 3 of 4 recommended (OK)

**Ad Strength:** Average

**Asset ratings:** 1 Best, 5 Good, 2 Learning, 2 Low

**Pin usage:** None (good)

**Headline diversity:** Moderate (4 categories)
- Missing: social proof, offers/promotions, CTA variety

**Finding:** Solid foundation. Replace Low-rated assets. Add social proof and offer headlines to improve diversity. Add 1 more description.

## Step 3: PMax Asset Evaluation

### Asset Group: "Anti-Aging Collection"

**Completeness score: 95/100**
- Headlines: 14/15
- Long headlines: 4/5
- Descriptions: 5/5
- Images landscape: 12/20
- Images square: 10/20
- Images portrait: 3/20
- Logos: 4 total (landscape + square)
- Video: 3/5
- Business name: set
- CTA: set

**Quality assessment:**
- Text coherence: Strong. Headlines and descriptions work well in random combinations.
- Image variety: Weak. All 25 images are stock photos of generic skincare models. No actual GlowLab product photography. No lifestyle imagery showing the product in use.
- Video quality: Good. Three professionally produced 15-second product demos.
- Logo clarity: Good. Clear at small sizes.

**Theme coherence:** Strong. "Anti-aging skincare for women 35-55 concerned about fine lines and wrinkles." Clear, focused theme.

**Finding:** Near-complete asset set with strong text and video, but image assets are entirely stock photography. Replace stock photos with actual product imagery and lifestyle shots. This is the single biggest quality improvement available for this asset group.

### Asset Group: "Suncare Essentials"

**Completeness score: 40/100**
- Headlines: 4/15 (Warning: just above minimum)
- Long headlines: 1/5 (minimum only)
- Descriptions: 2/5 (minimum only)
- Images landscape: 3/20
- Images square: 2/20
- Images portrait: 0/20
- Logos: 2 total
- Video: 0/5 (Critical: Google is auto-generating video)
- Business name: set
- CTA: set

**Quality assessment:**
- Text coherence: Weak. Only 4 headlines, limited combinations available.
- Image variety: Very weak. 5 total images, all product shots on white background.
- Video quality: N/A. No video uploaded. Google auto-generating from limited images.
- Logo clarity: OK.

**Theme coherence:** Unclear. Asset group contains both sunscreen and after-sun products without a clear audience focus.

**Finding:** This asset group is severely under-resourced. It's running with serious handicaps across every asset type. Priority actions: upload at least 1 video, add 7+ headlines, add 3 descriptions, add 10+ images across all ratios. Clarify theme focus (sunscreen vs. after-sun, or split into two groups).

## Step 4: Video Creative Evaluation

### YouTube Awareness Campaign: "GlowLab Brand Awareness"

**Video ad: 15-second non-skippable**

**ABCD Assessment:**
| Element | Rating | Notes |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Attention | Present | Opens with close-up of product application, movement in frame |
| Branding | Partial | Logo appears at end (second 13-15), but NOT in first 5 seconds. No audio brand mention. |
| Connection | Present | Shows relatable morning routine scenario with real person |
| Direction | Missing | No CTA. Video ends with logo but no "Visit glowlab.com" or "Shop now" |

**Format:** 15s non-skippable, 16:9. Appropriate for awareness objective.

**Fatigue signal:** CTR has declined from 2.8% to 1.9% over the past 4 weeks. Impressions stable at ~50K/week. This is a 32% CTR decline, well above the 20% fatigue threshold.

**Finding:** Video has strong attention and connection elements but is missing branding in the first 5 seconds and has no CTA at all. Additionally, the creative is showing clear fatigue signals. Needs a refresh with corrected branding placement and an added CTA.

---

## Step 5: Fatigue Detection

| Campaign | Signal | Detail |
|----------|--------|--------|
| YouTube Brand Awareness | CTR decline | 32% decline over 4 weeks (2.8% to 1.9%), stable impressions |
| YouTube Brand Awareness | Creative age | Same video running for 78 days (exceeds 60-day threshold) |
| PMax - Anti-Aging | Frequency (Display) | 6.2/week average (warning threshold: 5-7 for PMax Display) |

**Other campaigns:** No fatigue signals detected in Search (exempt) or PMax Suncare (insufficient volume to measure).

---

## Step 6: Cross-Campaign Consistency

| Check | Status | Detail |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Value proposition | Inconsistent | Search ads for Anti-Aging say "dermatologist recommended." PMax Anti-Aging asset group does not mention this claim at all. YouTube video doesn't reference it either. |
| Offer alignment | Consistent | "Free shipping over $50" appears in Search and PMax |
| Brand voice | Consistent | Professional, approachable tone across all campaigns |
| CTA consistency | Inconsistent | Search says "Shop Now." PMax says "Learn More." YouTube has no CTA. |

**Finding:** The "dermatologist recommended" claim appears only in Search RSAs. If this is a key differentiator (and it likely is, given it's a "Best" rated headline), it should appear across PMax headlines and in video creative. CTA inconsistency is minor but should be standardized.

## Outputs

### Summary Dashboard

**Creative Health Score: 52/100**

**Key Findings:**
1. Moisturizers RSA is effectively a static ad with 5 headlines, all pinned, and Poor ad strength. This is the most urgent fix.
2. Suncare PMax asset group at 40/100 completeness is running with major handicaps, including auto-generated video.
3. YouTube creative shows 32% CTR fatigue after 78 days with no refresh.
4. "Dermatologist recommended" claim appears only in Search, missing from PMax and YouTube.
5. Anti-Aging PMax has strong text but relies entirely on stock photography for images.

**Immediate Action Items (P1):**
- [ ] Rewrite Moisturizers RSA: add 10 headlines, remove all pins, target Good+ ad strength
- [ ] Upload at least 1 video to Suncare PMax asset group (stops auto-generation)
- [ ] Refresh YouTube creative: new video with branding in first 5 seconds and clear CTA

**Quick Wins:**
- [ ] Add "dermatologist recommended" headlines to PMax Anti-Aging and Suncare asset groups
- [ ] Replace 2 Low-rated assets in Anti-Aging Serums RSA
- [ ] Standardize CTA language across campaigns ("Shop Now" everywhere)

### Test Plan

**P1: Moisturizers RSA Rebuild**
- Variable: Complete RSA rewrite (new headlines, removed pins)
- Hypothesis: Diverse, unpinned headlines will improve CTR because Google can optimize combinations for each auction
- Method: Before/after (current RSA is too weak to split-test against)
- Duration: 14 days post-launch
- Success criteria: CTR improves 15%+ vs. prior 30-day baseline

**P2: Stock vs. Product Photography (PMax Anti-Aging)**
- Variable: Replace stock photos with actual product imagery
- Hypothesis: Authentic product photos will improve engagement because they set accurate expectations and build brand recognition
- Method: Add product photos as new assets, monitor asset performance labels over 30 days
- Duration: 30 days
- Success criteria: New product images earn Good or Best ratings, stock photos trend toward Low

**P2: YouTube Creative Refresh**
- Variable: New 15s video with corrected branding and CTA
- Hypothesis: Fresh creative will restore CTR to pre-fatigue levels because the audience has over-indexed on the current video
- Method: Launch new video, pause old video after 7 days
- Duration: 21 days
- Success criteria: CTR returns to 2.5%+ (within range of pre-fatigue 2.8%)

**P3: Suncare PMax Asset Completion**
- Variable: Complete asset group from 40/100 to 80+/100
- Hypothesis: A fully equipped asset group will improve impressions and conversions because Google can serve across more placements with appropriate creative
- Method: Add all missing assets, compare 30-day before vs. 30-day after
- Duration: 30 days
- Success criteria: Conversions increase 20%+ from Suncare asset group
