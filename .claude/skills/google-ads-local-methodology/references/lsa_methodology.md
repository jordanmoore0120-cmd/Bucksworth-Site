# Local Services Ads (LSA) Methodology

## Overview

Local Services Ads are a pay-per-lead ad format that appears above standard Search ads for eligible service businesses. Google verifies the business through background checks and license validation, and displays a "Google Guaranteed" or "Google Screened" badge. LSAs are fundamentally different from Search ads: ranking is based on reviews, responsiveness, and proximity rather than keyword bids.

---

## LSA Ranking Factors

### 1. Review Score and Volume (Most Important)
- Review score (4.0+ minimum to be competitive, 4.5+ to rank well)
- Review volume (more reviews = stronger ranking signal)
- Review recency (recent reviews weighted more heavily than old ones)
- Review content (Google may analyze review text for service relevance)
- Reviews on the LSA profile specifically, not just GBP reviews

### 2. Responsiveness to Leads
- Response time to new leads (faster is better)
- Answer rate for phone leads (missed calls hurt ranking)
- Message response rate (if messaging is enabled)
- Google tracks this automatically and factors it into ranking

### 3. Proximity to Searcher
- Distance between business location and searcher
- For service-area businesses: distance from the center of the defined service area
- Closer businesses rank higher, all else being equal

### 4. Business Hours and Availability
- Being available during peak search times improves ranking
- Extended hours (evenings, weekends) can provide an advantage in competitive markets
- Marking unavailable for extended periods reduces ranking

### 5. Budget
- Weekly budget (not daily)
- Higher budget = more lead opportunity, but budget alone does not determine ranking position
- Budget sets a cap on lead volume, not a bid on position

### 6. Complaint History
- Customer complaints filed through Google reduce ranking
- Unresolved complaints have a greater negative impact than resolved ones
- Consistent complaint patterns can lead to suspension

## LSA Bid Strategies

### Maximize Leads (Default)
- Google optimizes to deliver the maximum number of leads within your weekly budget
- No per-lead cost control
- Best for: businesses that want volume and can handle varying cost per lead

### Manual Bid Per Lead Type
- Set a maximum cost-per-lead for different service categories
- Example: "roof repair" leads capped at $80, "gutter cleaning" leads capped at $30
- Provides cost control but may reduce lead volume if bids are too low
- Best for: businesses with clear lead value differences across service types

### Budget Setting
- Budget is weekly, not daily
- Minimum budget varies by market and category
- Google may spend over budget on high-demand days and under on low-demand days
- Budget resets each week

## Lead Quality Management

### Types of Leads
- **Phone calls**: caller connects directly, tracked by duration
- **Messages**: text-based inquiries through the LSA platform
- **Bookings**: direct appointment scheduling (if enabled)

### Disputing Invalid Leads
Leads can be disputed within 30 days if they meet these criteria:
- Wrong phone number or spam call
- Service not offered by the business
- Location outside the service area
- Duplicate lead (same customer, same request)
- Not a genuine customer inquiry (solicitation, wrong business)

### Dispute Process
1. Open the LSA dashboard or app
2. Select the lead to dispute
3. Choose the dispute reason
4. Submit (Google reviews and credits if valid)
5. Credit applied to account within a few days

### Monitoring Dispute Rate
- Track dispute rate monthly (disputed leads / total leads)
- Rate above 20% indicates a problem: targeting too broad, wrong categories selected, or service area mismatch
- Consistently high dispute rates may trigger Google review of the account

## LSA vs Search: When to Use Each

### Use LSA When
- Business is in an eligible category
- Reviews are strong (4.0+ average, 10+ reviews)
- Pay-per-lead model is preferred (lower risk than pay-per-click)
- Business can respond to leads quickly (within minutes, not hours)
- Want to appear above Search ads in results

### Use Search When
- More control over keywords and ad messaging is needed
- Business is not in an LSA-eligible category
- Budget is very small (Search allows finer cost control)
- Specific landing pages or conversion paths are important
- Search term data is needed for keyword research

### Run Both When
- Maximum local coverage is the goal
- Budget supports both channels
- LSA captures high-intent "Google Guaranteed" leads
- Search captures keyword-specific intent and supports remarketing
- No cannibalization: LSA and Search ads appear in different positions

## LSA Profile Optimization

### Profile Completeness
- Fill every available field (incomplete profiles rank lower)
- Business name must match GBP and legal records exactly
- Phone number must be a local or business number (not personal cell if possible)
- Website URL should match the GBP listing

### Service Categories
- Select all relevant service categories (but only categories you actually serve)
- More specific categories perform better than broad ones
- Review category performance regularly and remove underperforming categories

### Verification Documents
- Upload license and insurance documents promptly during setup
- Expired documents can pause the profile
- Set reminders to update documents before expiration

### Photos
- Professional headshot of the business owner or team
- Business photos (vehicles, equipment, completed work)
- Avoid stock photos (Google may reject them)
- 5+ photos minimum for a complete profile

### Business Description
- Describe services clearly and specifically
- Include service area and specialties
- Mention years of experience, certifications, or differentiators
- 300+ characters recommended

### Service Area
- Define accurately (too broad = irrelevant leads, too narrow = missed opportunity)
- Match GBP service area
- Review and adjust based on lead quality by location

## LSA Reporting and KPIs

### Available Metrics
- Total leads by type (phone, message, booking)
- Cost per lead by service type
- Lead status (new, active, completed, archived)
- Weekly and monthly spend vs budget

### KPIs to Track
- **Cost per lead**: by service category. Compare to Search CPL for the same services.
- **Lead-to-booking rate**: what percentage of LSA leads become customers
- **Dispute rate**: target under 10%, flag if over 20%
- **Response time**: average time to respond to new leads
- **Review trajectory**: are reviews increasing in volume and maintaining score

### Reporting Limitations
- No keyword-level data (LSA does not use keywords)
- No impression share or competitive metrics comparable to Search
- Limited audience or demographic data
- Performance data available in LSA dashboard, not standard Google Ads reporting
