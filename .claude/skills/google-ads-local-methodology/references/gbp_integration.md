# Google Business Profile Integration with Google Ads

## Linking Requirements

### Prerequisites
- GBP must be verified and in good standing (no suspensions or policy violations)
- Business information must be accurate and complete
- Owner or manager access to the GBP listing is required

### How to Link
- Navigate to Google Ads > Account Settings > Linked Accounts > Google Business Profile
- Request link from within Google Ads (GBP owner must approve)
- One GBP listing can be linked to one Google Ads account or one MCC
- Multiple GBP locations can be linked under a single account

### MCC Linking
- Linking at the MCC level shares GBP access with all child accounts
- Preferred for agencies managing multiple accounts for the same business
- Individual account linking overrides MCC-level linking if both exist

## What Linking Enables

### Location Extensions
- Show business address, phone number, and directions button alongside ads
- Can display distance from the searcher to the business location
- Significant CTR improvement on local-intent queries (typically +5-15%)
- Available on Search, Display, and YouTube

### Location-Based Bid Adjustments
- Bid higher for users physically closer to the business
- Combine with radius targeting for layered precision
- Requires "Presence" location targeting for maximum accuracy

### Local Inventory Ads
- For retail businesses with physical inventory
- Shows product availability at nearby stores
- Requires Merchant Center local inventory feed in addition to GBP
- Drives "available nearby" visibility in Shopping results

### PMax Local Channels
- Maps placement (ads appear in Google Maps search and navigation)
- Local search placement (enhanced local pack visibility)
- Requires GBP-linked asset group in PMax campaign
- Enables "directions" and "call" actions directly from Maps

### Store Visit Conversion Tracking
- Eligibility requirements: multiple physical locations, 1000+ ad clicks per month, sufficient store visit data volume
- Google estimates store visits using location data from opted-in users
- Reported as modeled/estimated metric, not exact count
- Not available for all businesses. Google determines eligibility automatically.

## GBP Signals That Affect Ad Performance

### Review Score and Volume
- Higher review scores improve trust signals on ads with location extensions
- Review volume matters: 50+ reviews creates stronger social proof than 5 reviews at the same score
- Responding to reviews (positive and negative) signals active management
- Review score is the single most important LSA ranking factor

### Business Hours
- Ads can be scheduled to show only during open hours (recommended for most local businesses)
- After-hours ad visibility wastes budget if the business cannot respond
- Holiday hours must be kept current to avoid serving ads when closed

### Photos
- 20+ photos is the optimization target (exterior, interior, products/services, team)
- Businesses with more photos receive more direction requests and website clicks
- Photo quality matters: professional or high-quality photos outperform phone snapshots
- Regular photo updates signal an active listing

### Posts and Updates
- Weekly GBP posts signal active business management
- Posts can promote offers, events, or updates
- Posts expire after 7 days (events expire after the event date)
- Consistent posting correlates with improved local pack visibility

### Q&A Section
- Pre-populate with common customer questions and answers
- Unanswered questions from users look neglected
- Questions and answers are indexed by Google and can influence search visibility

### Service/Product Catalog
- List all services with descriptions and pricing (if applicable)
- Enables richer local ad formats and better category matching
- Products listed in GBP can appear in local inventory results

## GBP Optimization Checklist for Ads Performance

### Foundational (Must Have)
- [ ] All business information complete and accurate (name, address, phone, website)
- [ ] Primary category correctly selected (most specific option available)
- [ ] Secondary categories added (up to 9 additional categories)
- [ ] Hours of operation current, including special/holiday hours
- [ ] Business description written (750 characters, include key services and location)

### Engagement (Should Have)
- [ ] 20+ photos uploaded (exterior, interior, products/services, team)
- [ ] Responding to all reviews within 24-48 hours
- [ ] Weekly GBP posts published
- [ ] Q&A section populated with 10+ common questions
- [ ] Products or services listed with descriptions

### Advanced (Nice to Have)
- [ ] Booking/appointment link configured (if applicable)
- [ ] Messaging enabled and monitored
- [ ] Menu or service menu uploaded (restaurants, salons)
- [ ] Regular photo updates (monthly minimum)
- [ ] Video content uploaded

## Multi-Location Considerations

### Listing Management
- Each physical location needs its own verified GBP listing
- Consistent NAP (name, address, phone) across all locations and directories
- Location-specific descriptions highlighting what makes each location unique
- Per-location photo sets (do not reuse the same photos across locations)

### Google Ads Structure
- Create location groups in Google Ads for targeting and reporting
- Location groups allow location extensions for specific campaigns
- Track per-location performance to identify underperforming locations
- Budget allocation should reflect location-level opportunity, not equal distribution

### Common Multi-Location Issues
- Overlapping service areas between locations (creates internal competition)
- Inconsistent review scores across locations (weakest location drags down LSA performance)
- Centralized phone number instead of location-specific numbers (breaks call tracking)
- Shared GBP management with inconsistent update frequency across locations
