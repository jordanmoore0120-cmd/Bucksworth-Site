# Local Campaign Type Selection

## Overview

Local businesses have multiple campaign type options in Google Ads, each with different strengths. The right choice depends on the business model, conversion type, budget, and whether GBP and LSA are available. Most local advertisers should start with Search and layer additional types as budget and data allow.

---

## Local Search Campaigns

### What It Is
Standard Search campaigns built with a local keyword strategy and local extensions.

### Keyword Strategy
- **[service] near me**: high intent, broad geographic signal ("plumber near me")
- **[service] [city]**: geographic qualifier ("dentist Chicago")
- **[service] [neighborhood]**: hyperlocal ("pizza Brooklyn Heights")
- **[service] [zip code]**: less common but used by some searchers
- **Emergency/urgent variants**: "emergency plumber", "24 hour locksmith"

### Required Extensions
- Location extensions (requires GBP link)
- Call extensions (for phone-oriented businesses)
- Sitelink extensions (services, hours, reviews, directions)
- Structured snippets (service types, neighborhoods served)

### When to Use
- Default starting point for all local businesses
- When maximum keyword and bid control is needed
- When tracking specific search queries matters (search term visibility)
- Budget-constrained accounts where every dollar must be accountable

### Strengths
- Full control over keywords, bids, and ad copy
- Search term report visibility
- Predictable CPC and conversion costs
- Easy to understand and optimize

### Limitations
- Limited to Search network (no Maps, Display, YouTube)
- Manual effort to manage keyword lists
- Does not capture demand from non-search channels

## Local PMax (with GBP Asset Group)

### What It Is
Performance Max campaign with GBP-linked location assets, enabling ads across Maps, local search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, and Discover.

### Requirements
- GBP linked and verified
- Location asset group created with GBP connection
- Standard PMax assets (headlines, descriptions, images, optional video)
- Conversion tracking configured (calls, forms, or store visits)

### What It Enables
- Maps placement (ads in Google Maps search results and navigation)
- Local search enhanced placement (richer local pack presence)
- Display ads targeted to local geographic area
- YouTube pre-roll and in-feed ads in local area
- Gmail and Discover placements for local audiences

### When to Use
- Businesses wanting broad multi-channel local visibility
- Accounts with sufficient conversion data for Smart Bidding (15+ conversions/month)
- When Maps placement is valuable (retail, restaurants, service businesses)
- As a complement to Local Search (not a replacement)

### Strengths
- Widest reach across Google properties
- Automated optimization across channels
- Maps placement (unique to PMax with GBP)
- Lower management overhead than running separate campaigns per channel

### Limitations
- Limited transparency into per-channel performance
- Less control over keywords and placements
- Requires sufficient conversion volume to optimize effectively
- Can cannibalize branded Search traffic if not managed carefully

## Local Services Ads (LSA)

### What It Is
Pay-per-lead ads that appear above Search results for eligible service businesses. Google-verified with background checks and license verification.

### Eligibility
- Available categories: legal, home services (plumbing, HVAC, electrical, cleaning, etc.), health, financial, real estate, pet services, and expanding
- Not available in all markets (primarily US, Canada, UK, select EU markets)
- Business must pass Google's verification process (background checks, license validation)

### Key Differences from Search
- Pay per lead, not per click
- Google verifies the business (badge of trust)
- Appears above paid Search ads in results
- Ranking based on reviews, responsiveness, and proximity (not keyword bids)
- Limited ad customization (Google generates the ad from profile information)

### When to Use
- Service businesses in eligible categories
- Businesses with strong review profiles (4.0+ average, 10+ reviews minimum)
- When pay-per-lead model is preferred over pay-per-click
- As a complement to Search (not a replacement, as both can run simultaneously)

## Display for Local Awareness

### What It Is
Geo-fenced Display campaigns targeting users within a defined local area with visual banner ads.

### When to Use
- Brand awareness for new location openings
- Event promotion (limited-time offers, grand openings, seasonal events)
- Retargeting local website visitors
- When budget allows for awareness alongside direct response campaigns

### Configuration
- Geographic targeting: radius or zip code around business
- Audience targeting: affinity or in-market audiences relevant to the business
- Frequency caps: limit impressions per user per day (3-5 recommended)
- Responsive display ads with local imagery

### Strengths
- Very low CPC ($0.30-1.50 typical for local Display)
- High reach within geographic area
- Visual format for brand building

### Limitations
- Low conversion intent (awareness, not direct response)
- Requires strong creative to stand out
- Can be difficult to measure direct ROI

---

## Demand Gen for Local

### What It Is
Demand Gen campaigns (Gmail, YouTube, Discover) targeted to audiences within a geographic area.

### When to Use
- Businesses with strong visual appeal (restaurants, retail, events, fitness)
- When targeting specific audience segments within a local area
- Mid-funnel engagement (awareness to consideration)
- Complement to Search for businesses with visual products or experiences

### Configuration
- Geographic targeting within local radius or DMA
- Audience targeting: custom segments, in-market, lookalike
- Visual assets: images and video required
- Conversion tracking: may need to optimize for micro-conversions (site visits, content engagement) if lead volume is low

## Campaign Type Decision Matrix

| Business Need | Primary Recommendation | Secondary | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maximum control, direct response | Local Search | N/A | Default starting point for all local |
| Broad local visibility | Local PMax (with GBP) | Local Search + Display | PMax requires 15+ conversions/month |
| Lead generation (services) | LSA + Local Search | Local Search only | LSA if eligible and reviews are strong |
| Brand awareness, new location | Display (geo-fenced) | Local PMax | Time-limited awareness push |
| Visual product/service | Demand Gen (local) | Local PMax | Requires strong creative assets |
| Emergency services | Local Search + LSA | Local Search only | 24/7 ad scheduling, call-only format |
| Multi-location chain | Local PMax + Local Search | Local Search per location | Location groups for management |

## Budget Allocation Guidance

For a local business running multiple campaign types:
- **60-70% to Local Search**: direct response, highest conversion intent
- **15-25% to LSA**: if eligible, incremental lead volume
- **10-20% to PMax or Display**: broader reach and brand awareness
- Adjust based on performance data after 4-6 weeks
