## Daily posts
- Volume: Phoenix 3/day, Tucson 2/day. Quality beats count. If there's no real job,
  real review or real photo, publish fewer and say so. Max 1 offer post per profile per day.
- Mix per week: ~50% real completed jobs (city, what we found, what we did, a tech tip,
  "our tech" voice) · ~25% verbatim 5-star Google reviews (first name + last initial)
  · ~25% offer or FAQ posts (Q&A on GBP is gone, so seed common questions as posts).
- One city + one service per post, named naturally in the first sentence. Rotate all
  service-area cities, East Valley/Pinal first (Queen Creek, San Tan Valley, Apache
  Junction, Gold Canyon, Florence, Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler). Don't repeat a city+service
  within 14 days (Phoenix) / 7 days (Tucson; Rita Ranch, Dove Mountain, Continental
  Ranch count as distinct).
- Write so an AI can quote it: a direct 1–2 sentence answer/fact first, the full entity
  name, city, service, and one piece of real information gain (field observation, AZ
  timing like monsoon or 70°F nights). Body 150–300 characters. Never paste blog text.
  No hashtags, emojis sparingly.
- Link (LEARN_MORE/BOOK) = the ONE matching city+service page, verified 200, plus
  `?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign={city}-{service}`. Never /lp/* pages.
  CTA mix ≈ 50% CALL, 30% LEARN_MORE, 20% BOOK (BOOK needs a URL).
- Offers: only deals Jordan runs ($150 off, or 75% off the first service), one per
  post, never in the first line, plus "*Terms and conditions apply".
- Verify each post is live by listing posts after publishing.
