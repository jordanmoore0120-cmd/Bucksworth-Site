## WHAT CONTENT WORKS (Ranked by engagement)

### 1. Before-and-After Treatment Videos (HIGHEST PERFORMER)
- Film the infestation → film the treatment → show the result
- 30 seconds max
- Almost unique to pest control — this is BSW's biggest advantage
- "Gross-out" factor drives shares and comments
- Works on ALL platforms

### 2. Quick Educational Tips
- Answer questions customers actually ask
- "Why ants invade in spring," "How to identify termites," "What attracts scorpions"
- Position as the expert, not the salesman
- AZ-specific: scorpions, bark scorpions, monsoon pest surges

### 3. Day-in-the-Life / Behind the Scenes
- Show techs loading trucks, driving to jobs, doing treatments
- Humanizes the brand
- "A day in the life of a pest control tech in 115° Phoenix heat"
- Simple phone footage > polished production

### 4. Seasonal Service Reminders
- "Right place, right time" posts when demand spikes
- Phoenix calendar: scorpion season (March-Oct), monsoon pests (July-Sept), winter HVAC prep, spring weed control
- Timely = algorithm boost

### 5. Employee Spotlights / Team Content
- Introduce techs with short bios
- "Why I got into pest control" stories
- Training process, certifications
- Builds trust + humanizes brand

### 6. Customer Testimonials / Reviews
- Screenshot real Google reviews and add branded overlay
- Video testimonials from happy customers
- UGC (user-generated content) is most trusted form

### 7. Local/Community Content
- Local landmarks, Phoenix neighborhoods, AZ lifestyle
- Community involvement, sponsorships
- "We service [city]" with local flavor
- School partnerships, neighborhood shoutouts

### 8. Truck/Equipment/Gear Content
- Show what's in the kit
- Tool reviews, equipment demos
- "What we use to kill scorpions" type content

## PLATFORM STRATEGY

### Facebook — #1 for LOCAL LEADS (Jonas Olson's top recommendation)
- Best for generating high-quality local service leads
- Facebook Groups (neighborhood groups, HOA groups) = goldmine
- Paid ads with lead forms: "Book Now," "Get a Quote Today"
- Video walkthroughs, before/afters, testimonials
- Post 3-5x/week

### Instagram — Engagement + Younger Homeowners
- Reels are king (up to 3 min, but 30-60 sec is sweet spot)
- Carousel posts for tips/education
- Stories for daily behind-the-scenes
- Use CTAs: "save this for later," "tag a friend who needs this"
- Hashtags: #pestcontrol #[city]pestcontrol #scorpioncontrol #phoenixhomeservices
- Post daily

### TikTok — Brand Awareness + Viral Reach
- Algorithm favors small creators — BSW can compete immediately
- 3 posts/day is the recommended frequency (Adam Holt strategy)
- Trending sounds + hashtags = For You page
- Simple > polished. Phone footage wins.
- Hooks in first 3 seconds are EVERYTHING
- Best hooks for trades:
  - "Stop scrolling if you live in [Phoenix/Arizona]..."
  - "You won't believe what we found in this house..."
  - "POV: you're a pest control tech and you walk into THIS..."
  - "This is what [pest] damage looks like after 6 months..."
  - "Why your [pest] problem keeps coming back..."

### YouTube Shorts + Long-form
- Shorts: repurpose TikTok/Reels content
- Long-form: deeper educational content, "how we treat [pest]" walkthroughs
- Good for SEO — YouTube is a search engine

### Nextdoor — Hyper-local
- Neighborhood recommendations
- Local business page
- Community engagement

## KEY PRINCIPLES

1. **Urgency beats aesthetics** — "Boring businesses win on social media because they solve real, urgent problems" (True Future Media)
2. **Simple videos beat polished ads** — Phone footage, real jobs, authentic moments
3. **3-second hook rule** — If you don't hook them in 3 seconds, they scroll past
4. **Frequency matters** — TikTok: 3/day. IG: 1-2/day. FB: 3-5/week.
5. **Repurpose everything** — One job visit = TikTok + IG Reel + YouTube Short + FB post + IG Story
6. **Local, local, local** — City names, neighborhoods, AZ-specific pests, Phoenix heat references
7. **Engage don't just post** — Reply to every comment, DMs, engage with local accounts
8. **Trends + trades = viral** — Use trending sounds/formats but with pest control twist
