## What to post (priority order)
1. Before/after treatment videos from real jobs
2. Quick Arizona-specific tips (scorpion season, monsoon pests, pre-emergent timing)
3. Day-in-the-life / behind the scenes with our techs
4. Seasonal reminders
5. Employee spotlights
6. Real customer review highlights (verbatim, first name + last initial)
7. Local community content

## Rules
- A 3-second hook first. Real phone footage beats studio production.
- Repurpose each job into a TikTok, Reel, Short and Facebook post.
- Hyper-local: rotate service cities and neighborhoods (East Valley/Pinal first).
- Facebook = strongest local-lead channel. Instagram = Reels/Stories. TikTok = reach.
  YouTube Shorts = search value (AI Overviews often cite YouTube).
- Same photo rules as GBP: real field photos, no competitor logos, no Jordan Air, no
  old triangle logo, no customer faces or house numbers.
