# Creative Formats - Specs, Best Practices, and Performance Benchmarks

## Format Specifications (2026)

### Static Image Ads

**Recommended specs:**
- Feed: 1080 x 1080px (1:1) or 1080 x 1350px (4:5)
- Stories/Reels: 1080 x 1920px (9:16)
- Right column: 1200 x 628px (1.91:1)
- File type: JPG or PNG
- Max file size: 30MB
- Text on image: Keep under 20% of image area (not enforced but still impacts delivery)

**Best practices:**
- Lead with a clear visual hierarchy: one dominant element, one supporting element
- Use high contrast colors that stand out in the feed
- Include social proof numbers when possible (4.8 stars, 10,000+ users, $2M saved)
- Text overlays should be readable at mobile size (minimum 24pt equivalent)
- CTA can be on the image or in the ad copy -- test both

**Performance benchmarks (typical):**
- CTR: 0.8-1.5%
- Hook rate: 20-30%
- Shelf life: 2-4 weeks
- Production time: 1-4 hours

**Best for:**
- Direct response with clear offer
- Retargeting (product reminder)
- Comparison/competitive messaging
- Testimonial quotes with headshot
- Feature announcements

### Video Ads (General)

**Recommended specs:**
- Feed: 1080 x 1080 (1:1) or 1080 x 1350 (4:5)
- Stories/Reels: 1080 x 1920 (9:16)
- In-stream: 1920 x 1080 (16:9)
- Duration: 6-30 seconds (15 seconds is the sweet spot)
- Max duration: 241 minutes (but don't)
- File type: MP4 or MOV
- Max file size: 4GB
- Captions: Required (85% of feed video is watched without sound)

**General video best practices:**
- Hook in first 3 seconds (this is non-negotiable)
- Design for sound-off first, sound-on as bonus
- Use captions/text overlays throughout
- End with a clear CTA (verbal + visual)
- Native aspect ratio for placement (9:16 for Stories, 1:1 or 4:5 for Feed)

### UGC Video

**What it is:** User-generated content style video. May be actual user content or creator-produced content that looks organic.

**Characteristics:**
- Shot on phone (selfie mode or handheld)
- Natural lighting, real environments
- Conversational tone, not scripted-sounding
- Personal story or testimonial format
- Minimal branding (brand name mentioned verbally, logo at end only)

**Structure (15-30 seconds):**
1. Hook (0-3s): "I found this app that changed how our team works..."
2. Problem (3-8s): "We were drowning in Slack messages..."
3. Solution (8-18s): "Viktor handles all of it automatically..."
4. Result (18-25s): "We saved 10 hours last week alone"
5. CTA (25-30s): "Link in bio / Try it free"

**Performance benchmarks:**
- CTR: 1.0-2.0%
- Hook rate: 30-45%
- Hold rate: 45-60%
- Shelf life: 3-6 weeks
- Production time: 2-8 hours (with creator)

**Best for:**
- Cold prospecting (blends with organic feed)
- Social proof / testimonials
- Product demonstrations in real context
- Before/after stories

### Polished/Brand Video

**Characteristics:**
- Professional production (lighting, camera, editing)
- Brand-consistent colors, fonts, music
- Voiceover or professional audio
- Motion graphics and transitions
- Higher production value

**Performance benchmarks:**
- CTR: 0.6-1.2%
- Hook rate: 15-25%
- Hold rate: 50-65%
- Shelf life: 4-8 weeks
- Production time: 1-4 weeks

**Best for:**
- Brand awareness campaigns
- Product launches
- Consideration stage (longer content)
- Retargeting (reinforces brand quality)

**Warning:** Polished video often underperforms UGC for direct response. Use polished for brand, UGC for conversion.

### Founder/Talking Head Video

**Characteristics:**
- Founder or team member speaking directly to camera
- Casual but intentional setting (office, home office, interesting background)
- Authentic, personal, and authoritative
- Can be iPhone-shot or lightly produced

**Structure (15-45 seconds):**
1. Hook (0-3s): "I'm [Name], I built [Product] because..."
2. Why (3-15s): The personal story or insight
3. What it does (15-30s): Quick demo or explanation
4. Social proof (30-40s): "X thousand teams use it now"
5. CTA (40-45s): "Try it free, link below"

**Performance benchmarks:**
- CTR: 1.2-2.5%
- Hook rate: 35-50%
- Hold rate: 50-65%
- Shelf life: 4-8 weeks
- Production time: 1-4 hours

**Best for:**
- SaaS / B2B (builds trust)
- Early-stage companies (founder story resonates)
- Contrarian or thought-leadership angles
- When trust is the conversion barrier

### Carousel Ads

**Recommended specs:**
- Cards: 2-10 cards
- Image size per card: 1080 x 1080 (1:1)
- Video per card: Up to 15 seconds each
- File type: JPG, PNG, MP4, MOV
- Headline per card: 40 characters recommended

**Best practices:**
- First card must hook (it's your "ad")
- Tell a sequential story across cards (don't make each card independent)
- Use the last card as a CTA
- Test "See More" teasers on the right edge of each card to encourage swiping
- 5-6 cards is the sweet spot (enough story, not too long)

**Carousel story structures:**
1. Problem > Pain Points > Solution > Features > CTA
2. Before > The Change > After > Proof > CTA
3. "5 Reasons Why..." (one per card) > CTA
4. Product Feature 1 > Feature 2 > Feature 3 > Feature 4 > CTA
5. Testimonial 1 > Testimonial 2 > Testimonial 3 > CTA

**Performance benchmarks:**
- CTR: 1.0-2.0%
- Hook rate: 25-35%
- Swipe-through rate: 15-30% (reach last card)
- Shelf life: 3-5 weeks
- Production time: 2-6 hours

### Collection Ads

**What it is:** A cover image/video that opens into an Instant Experience (full-screen mobile landing page) with product catalog below.

**Specs:**
- Cover: Image (1080 x 1080) or video (up to 120 seconds)
- Instant Experience: Template-based or custom
- Products: Pulled from catalog (minimum 4 products)

**Best for:**
- E-commerce product discovery
- Multi-product showcases
- Reducing friction (users browse without leaving Meta)

**Not recommended for:** SaaS, lead gen, single-product businesses

### Catalog / Dynamic Product Ads (DPA)

**What it is:** Automated ads that pull products from your catalog feed. Meta shows the most relevant products to each user based on their browsing/purchase behavior.

**Types:**
- Retargeting DPA: Shows products people viewed but didn't buy
- Broad DPA: Shows products to cold audiences based on predicted interest
- Catalog Sales: Full catalog browsing experience

**Specs:**
- Product images from catalog feed
- Titles and descriptions from feed
- Pricing from feed
- Overlay templates available (sale badges, percentage off, free shipping)

**Best practices:**
- Feed quality is everything: high-res images, accurate titles, correct pricing
- Use lifestyle images over white-background product shots when possible
- Apply overlays for promotions (Meta has built-in templates)
- Segment catalogs for different campaigns (bestsellers, new arrivals, clearance)

## Barry Hott "Ugly Ads" Deep Dive

### The Philosophy

Over-produced ads look like ads. People have developed "ad blindness" to polished creative. Ugly ads look like organic content posted by a friend. They bypass the mental filter.

### Characteristics of Effective Ugly Ads

| Element | Polished Ad | Ugly Ad |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| Camera | Professional | iPhone (front or back) |
| Lighting | Studio or professional | Natural / ambient |
| Setting | Staged or on-brand | Real desk, real kitchen, real office |
| Audio | Professional VO or music | Voice memo quality, room echo OK |
| Text overlay | Brand fonts, animated | CapCut/Instagram Stories native fonts |
| Branding | Logo prominent | Logo absent or tiny at end |
| Editing | Smooth transitions | Jump cuts, raw footage feel |
| Talent | Models or actors | Real users, team members, founder |
| Script | Polished copy | Conversational, unscripted feel |

### When Ugly Ads Work Best

- Cold prospecting on Instagram and Facebook Feed
- UGC testimonial format
- "I just discovered this thing" narrative
- Problem-agitation-solution storytelling
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Quick demos or walkthroughs

### When Ugly Ads Don't Work

- Luxury or premium products (brand perception matters)
- Retargeting (audience already knows you, polish reinforces trust)
- Brand awareness campaigns
- B2B enterprise (can work for SMB B2B like Viktor)

### The Quality Test

"Ugly" does NOT mean "lazy" or "bad." Apply this filter:

**Could this ad profitably spend $10K?**

If yes: the ad has strategic quality even if production quality is low.
If no: it's just a bad ad, not an ugly ad.

**Ugly ad quality checklist:**
- [ ] Clear hook in first 3 seconds
- [ ] Single, focused message
- [ ] Speaks to a real pain point
- [ ] Social proof or credibility element
- [ ] Clear CTA
- [ ] Watchable without sound (captions)
- [ ] Authentic feel (would not look out of place in organic feed)

## Format Selection by Funnel Stage

| Funnel Stage | Primary Formats | Secondary Formats |
|-------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Awareness | Video (UGC, brand), Reels | Carousel (educational) |
| Consideration | UGC testimonial, Founder video, Carousel | Static (comparison) |
| Conversion | Static (offer), UGC (testimonial), Demo video | Carousel (features) |
| Retargeting | Static (reminder), DPA, Carousel | Video (social proof) |
| Retention | Static (upsell), Email (not Meta) | Carousel (new features) |
