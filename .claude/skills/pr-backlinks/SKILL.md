# PR + backlinks (Bucksworth)

Read `bucksworth-rules` first.

## Why
Authority/prominence is the ranking bottleneck. In 2026-06, CI Web Group's Cortex
review put Domain Rating at 0. AI answers cite local news, community sites and lists,
so mentions count as well as links. Jun–Aug 2026: 435 generic emails (directories,
HVAC guest-post farms) landed **0 links**. Don't repeat that. Go for quality and
locality over volume.

## Verified earned link
FOX 10 Phoenix "Seen on TV: August 24" (fox10phoenix.com/news/seen-tv-august-24-2026)
links to getyourbucksworth.com (followed, indexed; checked 2026-09-22). Proof line:
"As seen on FOX 10 Phoenix".

## Rules
- Pitch pest control, scorpions, termites, rodents and weed control ONLY. Never AC/HVAC/
  plumbing, prices, "no contract", "#1", awards or customer counts. Founded 2013,
  Arizona family-owned, owner-operated, not a franchise, not private-equity owned.
- Send from / CC `info@getyourbucksworth.com`. Sign as Jordan Moore, Founder & Owner,
  Bucksworth Home Services. Never Jordan's cell, never jordan@.
- **Journalist pitches and press releases:** draft → post in `#bucksworth-digital` → send
  only after Jordan OKs. Partnership/resource-page/sponsorship asks can go directly.
- Anything that costs money (membership, sponsorship, paid listing): list it with the
  price for Jordan. Never commit to it.
- Ask for links to the matching CITY PEST PAGE (e.g. /queen-creek-az/pest-and-termite),
  verified 200, not just the homepage.
- About 10 new emails/day max, plus follow-ups at 7 and 14 days (max 2). Never re-pitch a
  finished sequence unless there's a genuinely new angle.

## Targets (East Valley / Pinal first)
1. Apache Junction, Gold Canyon, Queen Creek, San Tan Valley, Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler,
   Florence: chambers, HOAs/master-planned community vendor lists and newsletters,
   youth sports/school sponsorships, community event pages, local news (AJ Independent,
   East Valley Tribune, Queen Creek Tribune, San Tan Sun News, InMaricopa, Pinal Central,
   Gilbert Sun News, Mesa Tribune).
2. TV/radio with the FOX 10 proof and seasonal angles: winter roof rats (Oct–Jan),
   termite inspections before home sales, pre-emergent timing, scorpions after the first
   cold snap. Outlets: ABC15, 12News/azfamily, KJZZ, KTAR, Rosie on the House.
3. Supplier/manufacturer "find a pro" pages, the AZ pest association (AzPPO), BBB, and
   real-estate agent and home-inspector resource pages.

getyourbucksworth.com. Note whether it's dofollow and whether the page is indexable.
