# Campaign Structure Templates by Business Model

## SaaS / B2B (Viktor)

### Starter Structure ($5K-15K/month)

```
Account: Viktor
├── SALES_TESTING_ABO_2026-03 (25% budget, ~$125-375/day)
│   ├── AS: BROAD_US_ALLPLACEMENTS ($100-200/day)
│   │   └── 6-12 test ads (mix of UGC, founder, demo)
│   └── AS: INTEREST_AI-SLACK-TOOLS_ALLPLACEMENTS ($50-150/day)
│       └── 6-12 test ads
│
├── SALES_WINNERS_CBO_2026-03 (60% budget, ~$300-900/day)
│   └── AS: BROAD_US_ALLPLACEMENTS
│       └── 5-8 proven winners (Post ID from testing)
│       └── Cost Cap: Target CPA + 20%
│
└── SALES_RETARGET_ABO_2026-03 (15% budget, ~$75-225/day)
    └── AS: RETARGET_WEB-30D_ALLPLACEMENTS
        └── 3-5 retargeting-specific ads
        └── Exclude: purchasers, registered users
```

**Notes:**
- Keep it simple. Three campaigns maximum.
- All prospecting creative funnels through Testing before entering Winners.
- Retargeting runs separately with dedicated creative (testimonials, demos, reminders).

## Structure Migration Guide

### From Fragmented to Consolidated

**Before (fragmented, common mistake):**
```
10 campaigns:
├── LAL 1% Campaign
├── LAL 2% Campaign
├── Interest Stack A Campaign
├── Interest Stack B Campaign
├── Interest Stack C Campaign
├── Broad Campaign
├── Retarget 3-Day Campaign
├── Retarget 7-Day Campaign
├── Retarget 14-Day Campaign
└── Brand Awareness Campaign
```

**After (consolidated):**
```
3 campaigns:
├── SALES_TESTING_ABO
│   └── 2-3 ad sets for structured testing
├── SALES_WINNERS_CBO
│   └── 1-2 ad sets (broad + optional LAL)
└── SALES_RETARGET_ABO
    └── 1 ad set (combined 7-30 day)
```

**Migration steps:**
1. Identify your best-performing ads across all 10 campaigns
2. Collect their Post IDs
3. Create the new 3-campaign structure
4. Add the top performers via Post ID to the Winners campaign
5. Run old and new in parallel for 7 days
6. Compare total account performance (not campaign-by-campaign)
7. If new structure matches or beats old: pause old campaigns one at a time
8. Full migration should take 2-3 weeks
